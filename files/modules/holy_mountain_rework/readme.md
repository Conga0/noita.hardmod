# Holy Mountain Rework
Reworks Holy Mountain, removing the ability to cheese them easily with teleport bolt or fine digging placement, while also providing players with a new interesting choice to make when leaving the Holy Mountain.
## Changes
- Removed collapse trigger, Holy Mountain no longer collapses when you leave
- Instead, the Holy Mountain will collapse after a perk is taken
- There is a new crystal hidden behind a false-wall on the right of the exit that when destroy, will allow a perk to be taken without collapsing the mountain, but anger the gods
- Moves the left-most statue to block the entrance to the Holy Mountain

## What this means
The player now has 3 choices before leaving the Holy Mountain
1. Take a Perk

This is the basic/default choice, it simply allows you to progress normally.

2. Leave behind the perk

A player can do this if they value being able to return to Holy Mountain and tinker. A player must weigh the risk of entering the next biome without a perk, and also must consider if they play risky and get to low HP, they may not survive the trip back to the last Holy Mountain and then to the next one.

3. Break or move the crystal and take a perk

A player can do this if they value taking their perk, but also wish to keep the tinker zone, the cost of their greed is angering the gods. It allows players to take both options but also now must deal with Stevari, and potentially Skoude in future Holy Mountains. If the crystal is destroyed, the gods will be angered immediately. If it is simply moved, it will anger the gods only when you take a perk.

## Additional benefits of this design
- Players no longer feel as heavily punished by rolling bad perks. Idealy we would like to improve the perk pool and balance it in this mod, but in the event the player rolls bad perks, they can at least retain the mountain in exchange.
- Players can no longer accidentally collapse their mountain (this isn't common, but can be mildly frustrating).

## Drawbacks
- Does not apply to the Final Holy Mountain
- The Holy Mountain's collapse has a lot less flair
- The path into the collapsed zone is not consistently blocked
